| **SS4H3b** | **2** | The Trail of Tears is not a footnote to Little Bighorn, and neither is the reservation system. |

### Quarter 3 — 10 units, 16 lessons, **6 spare**

| Unit | Lessons | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **SS4H4a — abolition and suffrage** | **3** | Georgia names five people in one element: Anthony, **Douglass**, Stanton, **Truth**, **Tubman**. One lesson makes them a list. |
| **SS4H5c — battles** | **2** | Five named events including the Atlanta Campaign and Sherman's March, both of which happened where she lives. |
| **SS4H5d — the people who ran the war** | **2** | Six named people on two sides. |
| **SS4H6c — sharecropping** | **2** | Two things in one element: how slavery was replaced, *and* how newly won rights were taken back. They are separate mechanisms. |
| **SS4H6d — Jim Crow** | **2** | The laws, and the practices. Georgia names both. |
| The other 5 | 1 each | |

**Quarter 3 is the lightest quarter on purpose and this is what the room is for.** Gigi's standing rule is that the Black-American-educator requirement is *not a nice-to-have* on SS4H4, SS4H6c and SS4H6d. **Those three elements get seven of the sixteen lessons.**

## The video search, before any prose

**32 videos, verified at `https://www.youtube.com/oembed?url=...&format=json`, titles stored character for character. Never noembed.**

### ⚠️ The Black-American-educator requirement is not optional in Quarter 3

The Science Lab came up **0 of 48**. Quarter 1 came up **0 of 16** across eighteen searches, though three real videos on Black soldiers of the Revolution were found and recorded.

**Quarter 3 is a different field and there is no excuse available.** Douglass, Tubman, Truth, sharecropping, the taking of the vote, Jim Crow — **a search that comes up empty on these is a search that was not run properly.** Every search gets written down either way, and identity is judged from name and presentation only: **unknown is recorded as unknown, never as a gap closed.**

### Lessons most likely to force a re-cut

| Lesson | Risk |
|---|---|
| **32 · the cotton gin and slavery** | Most cotton-gin videos are about the machine and stop there |
| **37 · states' rights** | Heavily contested framing; needs a source that says plainly what the right was to do |
| **46 · taking the vote back** | Poll taxes and literacy tests are usually taught at high school |
| **48 · how long Jim Crow lasted** | Almost everything treats it as ended and finished |

**If four searches find nothing usable for a nine-year-old, the module gets re-cut here — not papered over with something adjacent.**
