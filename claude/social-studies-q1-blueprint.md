# Social Studies — Quarter 1 Blueprint

**16 lessons · 14 Georgia units · 4 modules of a fortnight each · Mondays and Wednesdays.**
**Topics only. No video has been searched for yet, and no prose is written until every lesson has one.**

*Revolution, the Constitution, and all of civics — because the government she is learning about is the one those two events produced. Civics taught anywhere else is trivia.*

## Module 1 · The Road to Revolution — weeks 1–2

| # | Lesson | Unit | The one idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **The war before the war, and who got the bill** | SS4H1a | Britain won the French and Indian War and it cost a fortune. The Stamp Act was the bill arriving — and "no taxation without representation" is what you say when someone spends your money without asking you. |
| **2** | **The people who refused** | SS4H1a | Sons of Liberty, **Daughters of Liberty**, the Boston Massacre, the Boston Tea Party. Protest is a thing ordinary people organise, not something that just happens. |
| **3** | **Who was on which side** | SS4H1b | King George III, Washington, Franklin, Jefferson, Patrick Henry, John Adams, Paul Revere, **Benedict Arnold — who changed sides** — and **the Black regiments Georgia names by name.** |
| **4** | **Lexington and Concord** | SS4H1c | The morning the arguing stopped and the shooting started. |

| # | Lesson | Unit | The one idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| **5** | **The Declaration of Independence** | SS4H1d | Written a year *after* the war began, not before it. Who wrote it, how, and that most of it is a **list of accusations against a king**. |

## The video search, before any prose

**Sixteen videos, verified at `https://www.youtube.com/oembed?url=...&format=json`, title stored character for character. Never noembed** — at v3.9 it "verified" a deliberately fake id.

### ⚠️ This subject is not the field The Science Lab searched

The Science Lab came up **0 of 48** on Black American educators across 33 searches, in a field dominated by a few large animation channels. **That excuse does not transfer here.** Georgia's own text for this quarter names:

- **SS4H1b — Black regiments**, by name, in the standard
- **SS4H2b — the Three-Fifths Compromise**
- **SS4CG1b — "We the People"**, which invites exactly the question of who was excluded

**A search that comes up empty on these is a search that was not run properly.** Every search gets written down either way, and channel identity is judged from name and presentation only — **unknown is recorded as unknown, never as a gap closed.**

### The three lessons most likely to force a re-cut

| Lesson | Risk |
|---|---|
| **6 · Natural rights** | Abstract, and most videos on it are made for high school civics |
| **11 · "We the People"** | Same, plus the harder half — who was excluded — is usually a separate video |
| **16 · The First Amendment** | Plenty exists; almost all of it is pitched at teenagers |

**If a fourth search finds nothing usable for a nine-year-old, the module gets re-cut here — not papered over with something adjacent.**
