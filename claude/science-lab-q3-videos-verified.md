### ⚠️ Lesson 41 nearly failed the distinctness test

**Three searches for a video about the LIMITATIONS of solar-system models returned nothing usable for a child** — two motion-graphics pieces and no teaching video. Under this project's own rule that means *the lesson is not distinct enough* and Module 7 gets re-cut.

**The fourth search found `To Scale: THE SOLAR SYSTEM`** — a short film of a group building a scale model seven miles across in the Nevada desert, made for precisely the reason this lesson exists: no model that fits in a room can be honest about size and distance at once.

**Two caveats, in the file rather than buried here:** it is made for a general audience rather than for children, and it runs about seven minutes against a thirty-minute block. **It closes the lesson; the two beats do the teaching.** If Gigi wants it shorter, Lesson 41 gets re-cut around the lesson rather than around the video.

**It carries no `coverageNote`, and the check was right to refuse one** — the video covers three of the lesson's four words, so an exemption would be a waiver that is not needed, and a stale exemption is how a rule quietly stops applying to anything.
