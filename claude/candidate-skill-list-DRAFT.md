*Generated Aug 19 2026 from the lessons on disk. **Nothing is committed to the app.** This is Phase 1 of the tagging job, and §5.4 makes it the gate: "Skill list first — nothing else starts until it exists."*

## How this was derived, and why it is not a glossary

Every lesson carries a `words:` list — **1,024 entries, 801 unique**. Most of them are not skills:

| Appears on | Terms | What that means |
|---|---|---|
| 1 lesson | **645** | Vocabulary. Taught once, never returned to. **Not a skill.** |
| 2 lessons | 115 | Candidate |
| 3 lessons | 23 | Strong candidate |
| 4+ lessons | 18 | **The real spine** |

**A skill is a thing that recurs.** §3.2: *"Skills — not lessons — are what get mastered, reviewed, remediated and reported on."* A term taught in one lesson and never again is something she met, not something she has. So the 645 are excluded and **156 candidates** remain.

**Cut freely.** A shorter list that is true beats a longer one that flatters the content.
