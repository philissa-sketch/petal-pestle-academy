## ⚠️ The one error that is on two pages

Both the master calendar and the mathematics plan say some version of:

> *"Geometry and Measurement sit at the floor of 2nd while Fractions measured 3.89"*

**That was true on Aug 16 and it is not true now.** On Aug 13 both bottomed out at **2.00**, which was the item bank running out of room rather than a reading of her. The bank was extended, she was re-measured, and both are now real numbers:

| | On the printed page | On her record today |
|---|---|---|
| Geometry | 2.00 *(floor)* | **2.67** |
| Measurement & Data | 2.00 *(floor)* | **2.44** |
| Fractions & Decimals | 3.89 | 3.89 ✓ |

⚠️ **Do not read that as growth.** A floor is not a measurement — "she is at 2.00" meant "she is at or below 2.00, we cannot tell." Most of the movement is the instrument improving, not the child. **The page is wrong in a way that flatters, which is the worst direction.**

## ⚠️ `year-plan-01-mathematics` — the unit order was overturned

The page says **"Q1 — 2nd grade math, all 8 units"**, in order, and that was the design until **v3.81 eight days ago**.

The app no longer walks the course in order. **Each maths strand walks its own lane:**

| Strand | Level | Its lane | Opens at |
|---|---|---|---|
| Measurement & Data | 2.44 | units 5, 6, 7 | **Unit 5 · Money and time** |
| Geometry | 2.67 | unit 8 | **Unit 8 · Geometry** |
| Patterns & Early Algebra | 2.98 | *no lane* | Unit 1 · Add and subtract within 20 |

Before that change, all three were handed **Unit 1** — which teaches Numbers & Operations, her *second strongest* strand at 3.48. The plan's "all 8 units in order" is what produced that.

**She still covers all eight units across the year.** The page is not wrong about the destination; it is wrong about the route, and the route is the part that stopped her weakest strands being worked on.

## ⚠️ What is missing from ALL NINE pages

Three features were built after these were printed, and **not one page mentions any of them**:

- **The reading check** — v3.80. The pages correctly say Khan's elementary ELA has no assessment; they do not say the app now has one. **This is the tool that fills the largest blank on her record.**
- **The book reports** — v3.82 and v3.83. Four a year, four weekly steps, six formats, three writing boxes.
- **One grading ladder** — v3.84. Thirteen bands, A+ at 97%, one table for Khan units and book reports alike.

**None of that makes a page wrong. It makes them incomplete**, and a year plan handed to a homeschool reviewer that omits three graded activities understates the year.

---

## ⚠️ One divergence that is not an error

Every page carries dates — *"Q1 Aug 3 – Oct 31 · 58 days · ~12 wk."*

**The app has no calendar and refuses one.** Master plan §7.1: *"a quarter is a sequence, not a set of dates."* The pages and the app therefore describe the year in two different languages.

**That is probably right and should stay.** Georgia wants days and dates; a nine-year-old needs the next lesson. **Just do not expect the app to agree with the paper**, and do not "fix" one to match the other.
