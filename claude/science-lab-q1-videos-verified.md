### Two findings recorded as what they are, not as gaps closed

**1. `STEM with Mr N`** — an individual creator, surfaced on the pulley search. **Identity was not established either way and is recorded as unknown.** Channel identity is judged from name and presentation only, and a guess is not a record. Its video is also an activity rather than a lesson on what a pulley is, so it would not have suited Lesson 15 regardless.

**2. `Ubongo Kids English` (`3ti_50Qg71o`) and `Super Sema` (`5axEfQpb9xI`)** — both have a *Refraction Of Light In Water* video that maps well onto Lesson 23. **These are African productions — Ubongo is Tanzanian, Super Sema is Kenyan-set — which is Black-led educational media and is NOT the same thing as a Black American educator.** The standing requirement says Black American. They are recorded here as a real find, described accurately, and **flagged to Gigi as hers to decide**, rather than silently substituted or silently dropped.

**The honest reading, unchanged from v3.9:** the gap is not in Black expertise. Elementary *physical science* video on YouTube is dominated by a small number of large animation-led channels — AumSum, Dr. Binocs, Learn Bright, TutWay, SciShow Kids — and individual educators of any background barely register in the results at all.

**And YouTube's results pane returned only two videos per search in these sessions**, so this was not an exhaustive survey. A later pass with a fuller results page may find better, and may find the source these searches did not.
