PETAL & PESTLE ACADEMY
======================

HOW TO START IT
---------------
Double-click  START-PETAL-PESTLE.bat

The first time you do this it will spend a few minutes downloading what it
needs. That happens once, and it needs internet for that part only. After
that it works offline.

A black window opens. Leave it open while she works. Closing it stops the app.
Your browser opens on its own at http://localhost:5180

If Node.js is not installed, the black window will tell you and give you the
link. Install it (pick the big LTS button), then double-click the .bat again.


WHAT'S IN VERSION 1
-------------------
  The Greenhouse    her home screen
  Check-In          the adaptive diagnostic, ~12 questions per sitting
  My Levels         her results, drawn as a garden
  My Plan           exactly which Khan Academy course + unit to open, in order
  Market            the store - Petals and Golden Seeds
  My Greenhouse     her room, her shelves, and her - where everything she buys
                    actually appears
  Herb Library      24 plant cards
  Grown-Up Corner   passcode-locked: real numbers, full history, printable
                    report, and the reward approvals

Lessons are NOT in version 1. That was the decision: she takes the Check-In
first, and the lesson build is shaped by what it finds.


DR. MARIGOLD
------------
Her mentor, the way Lamar has Commander Nova. A Black American woman who is both
a physician and a herbalist - which is exactly what your granddaughter says she
wants to be.

She appears once on each screen and says ONE thing, always about what to do next.
She never tells her she is clever - praise aimed at ability makes a child avoid
hard things, which is the one behaviour that would wreck a diagnostic.

She is NEVER on screen during a question. A mentor looking over a child's
shoulder mid-test adds pressure to the one activity that has to feel safe.

She also carries a daily line - a quote or a thought, the same one all day.


MY GREENHOUSE
-------------
The place everything she buys actually goes. Three views:

  THE GREENHOUSE - a drawn room in perspective. Buy the stool and the stool
  appears in the room. Buy the rose arch and it grows over the back wall. Things
  she has NOT bought are drawn as dotted outlines standing in their own spots,
  with the price underneath, so she can see what her Petals are for.

  APOTHECARY SHELF - three real shelves. Jars, the mortar and pestle, the brass
  scales, the microscope. Same dotted outlines for the gaps.

  YOU - her avatar, drawn as a Black girl, in layers. The apron goes on, the
  gloves go on her hands, the straw hat sits on her head, the boots replace her
  shoes. Buying something wearable puts it on automatically, and she can change
  what she is wearing any time in the wardrobe.

Nothing here is locked behind a score. It is all bought with Petals, and Petals
come from effort.


THE STORE
---------
Two currencies, same idea as her brother's app.

  * PETALS - earned fast, spent in the app on greenhouse decorations, her
    apron and kit, and jars for her apothecary shelf. 32 things to collect.
    Costs you nothing. Never waits for anyone's permission.

  * GOLDEN SEEDS - the scarce one. Saved up for real-world rewards. Small
    privileges that cost you nothing (pick the film, stay up 30 minutes) clear
    on their own, up to 50 seeds a week. Everything else comes to you in the
    Grown-Up Corner as a request you approve or decline.

SHE EARNS FOR EFFORT, NOT FOR CORRECT ANSWERS. This was your call and it is
worth knowing why it matters: if being right paid more, she would work out
inside one sitting that hard questions cost her money, and start guessing safe
and skipping the strands she finds difficult. The Check-In would then be
measuring her strategy instead of her knowledge - and the gaps it exists to
find are exactly what it would stop being able to see. A wrong answer pays
exactly what a right one pays, and the app tells her so on the Market screen.

  Every question answered      3 Petals + 1 Seed
  Every strand finished       25 Petals + 8 Seeds
  Every sitting completed     15 Petals + 5 Seeds
  Every day she comes back    10 Petals + 3 Seeds

The whole diagnostic works out to about 715 Petals and 232 Golden Seeds -
enough for six to eight things off her shelf and one real reward at the
200-seed tier.

DREAM REWARD: she picks one big thing to save toward, and you add 1 seed for
every 4 she saves. That match is what makes the 500-seed tier reachable, and it
teaches her what an employer match feels like years before anyone explains one.

THE REWARD LIST IS YOURS TO CHANGE. Grown-Up Corner -> Rewards -> Reward list.
Add your own, re-price anything, remove what does not fit. No code needed.

Seeds are never taken while she waits. A request you decline, or never get to,
costs her nothing.


"READ IT TO ME"
---------------
There is a button on every question that reads it out loud - the passage, the
question and all four answers. It uses your computer's own voice, so nothing
goes over the internet.

Tell her to use it whenever she wants. It is there to be used, and the app says
so on the screen.

WHY IT MATTERS MORE THAN IT LOOKS: every question in every subject is made of
words. If a 6th-grade geometry question is written above her reading level, she
gets it wrong because she could not READ it - and the app records that as "weak
at geometry." Hearing the question removes that problem completely, and the math
strand goes back to measuring math.

The one place it changes the meaning: on Reading Comprehension and Vocabulary,
hearing the passage turns a reading question into a listening question. That is
still allowed - a child who needs it needs it - but your report will tell you
when it happened and how many, so you know which of those two numbers is a
listening estimate rather than a reading one.

Every question in the app was also rewritten to keep the reading load down.
Math questions are held to plain, short sentences no matter how hard the math
is. You can re-run that check any time with:  npm run check:reading


FIRST TIME SHE OPENS IT
-----------------------
1. It asks her name. That is all the setup there is.
2. Send her to Check-In. It takes about 15 minutes a sitting.
3. Full run is around 80 questions across roughly 7 sittings. She does not
   have to do them in one go, or on one day. It saves after EVERY question.
4. After the first sitting, open My Plan. It fills in as she goes.

The first time you open the Grown-Up Corner it asks you to set a passcode.


THINGS WORTH KNOWING
--------------------
* This is completely separate from her son's Mission Control app. Different
  folder, different database, different port. They can both be open at once
  and cannot touch each other.

* Everything is saved in the browser on THIS computer. There is no cloud copy.
  If browser data gets cleared, it is gone. The Grown-Up Corner has a
  "Download backup" button - use it now and then.

* The app does not teach Math, Reading or Science. Khan Academy does. This
  app's job is telling you where in Khan Academy to start.

* Khan Academy renames its units occasionally. If a unit name in My Plan stops
  matching the site, the course link still goes to the right course, and the
  names can be edited in one file: src/data/khan/khanMap.js


IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
------------------------
Open the black window's folder and run:  npm run check

That runs six built-in checks - the source files, the question bank, the reading
load, item placement, the diagnostic engine and the money rules - and prints
exactly what it finds.
